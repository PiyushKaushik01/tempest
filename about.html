About Us

For generations, the Kingdom of Tempest has stood where towering walls meet endless skies, a realm shaped not by conquest alone but by the people who call it home. Every road, fortress, and village carries the echoes of those who came before, while each new arrival quietly becomes another page in the kingdom's ever-growing story.

Deep beneath the royal castle lies the Sacred Registry, preserved within the ancient Royal Archives where countless names have been carefully recorded through the ages. More than a collection of records, it is the living memory of Tempest, safeguarding the legacy, achievements, and stories of every citizen who has ever walked its halls.

A kingdom is remembered not for its stone walls, but for the people who give those walls meaning. As Tempest continues to grow, every name entered into the Sacred Registry becomes part of a legacy that will endure for generations, ensuring that no contribution, no journey, and no story is ever forgotten.
